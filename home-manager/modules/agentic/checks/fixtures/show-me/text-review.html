Rollout plan

The rollout starts on the staging cluster at 09:00 UTC and moves to the production cluster once the readiness probe stays green for fifteen minutes. Operators watch the request error rate and the queue depth together; either crossing its threshold pauses the rollout.

Every batch is limited to ten percent of the fleet, and a batch that reports errors is held rather than retried automatically. Rollout pauses are announced in the operations channel before any manual step.

Rollback plan

Rollback re-points traffic to the previous release and keeps the new artifacts on disk for inspection. The database migration is backward compatible, so no schema reversal is required — the migration step is never destructive.

A rollback takes about four minutes and is triggered by the on-call engineer. Recorded reasons use plain text, for example <error budget exceeded>, and are pasted literally into the incident timeline.
